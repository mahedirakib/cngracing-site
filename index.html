CNG Racing

An arcade ride through everyday Dhaka traffic, for iPhone.

How to play

Tap Let's ride, then swipe left or right on the road to change lanes. Collect taka coins and avoid buses, rickshaws, CNGs, legunas, cars, motorbikes and people crossing. Vehicles blink their indicators before cutting across. Tap Honk to move nearby rickshaws aside; it recharges after a few seconds.

Common questions

There's no sound. Check the SOUND button in the top corner and your iPhone's Silent switch. The game respects Silent mode.

Where is my best score saved? On your iPhone only. Deleting the game resets it.

Is the map real? No. The street is a stylized composition inspired by Farmgate in Dhaka; shops and signs are fictional.
