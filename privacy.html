Privacy Policy

CNG Racing for iPhone · Effective 25 October 2026

In short: CNG Racing does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and the game works without an internet connection.

What stays on your iPhone

The game saves two settings on your device so they are there next time you play: your best distance and whether sound is switched on or off. They never leave your iPhone and are deleted when you delete the app.

What we do not collect

CNG Racing does not collect your name, contact details, location, device identifiers, usage data or any other information. It contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking software, and it does not use your camera, microphone, contacts or photos.

Children

Because no personal information is collected from anyone, none is collected from children.

Apple services

If you download the game from the App Store or test it through TestFlight, Apple handles that under its own privacy policy. Apple may share anonymous crash reports and usage statistics with developers only if you have chosen to share them in your iPhone's settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
